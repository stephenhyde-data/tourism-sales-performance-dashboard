
# Tourism Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project uses Tableau to analyse tourism product sales performance, customer demographics and conversion rates. The objective is to identify patterns in customer behaviour and highlight opportunities to improve sales performance.

## Dashboard Preview

![Tourism Sales Performance Dashboard](Tourism%20Sales%20Performance%20Dashboard%20%281%29.png)

## Key Performance Indicators

- **Total Customers:** 4,888
- **Products Taken:** 920
- **Overall Conversion Rate:** 18.8%

## Key Insights

- **Customer demographics:** Customers aged 26–46 account for approximately 74% of customers with recorded ages.
- **Contact type:** Company-invited customers achieved a 21.8% conversion rate, compared with 17.6% for self-enquiries.
- **City tiers:** Tier 3 customers recorded a 23.6% conversion rate, followed by Tier 2 at 23.2% and Tier 1 at 16.3%.
- **Passport ownership:** Customers with passports achieved a 34.7% conversion rate, compared with 12.3% for customers without passports.

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate opportunities to improve conversion among self-enquiry customers.
- Examine the factors contributing to stronger conversion rates in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities.
- Explore how passport ownership can inform customer segmentation and marketing strategies.
